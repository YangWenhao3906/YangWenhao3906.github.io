excerpt: "We study unfaithful claims in coding agents' work reports using real developer–agent conversations from SWE-chat. We identify eleven subtypes, measure the follow-up effort after users challenge a claim, and find that 37.24% of 435 sampled work reports contain at least one unfaithful claim. FaithDetector achieves 93.06% macro-F1 on naturally sampled reports."
